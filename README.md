# Ronith Reddy - Portfolio

Personal portfolio of **Ronith Reddy**, Front Creative Developer & Art Director.

Coming from a background in art direction and digital design, I later transitioned into front-end development and creative coding. This portfolio is the result of that journey, a fusion of visual direction, interaction design and creative development.

## About

The portfolio brings together selected projects across creative development, web design, branding and art direction.

The experience was designed and developed from scratch with a strong focus on:

- Interactive storytelling
- WebGL and Three.js experiences
- Motion and transitions
- Visual consistency across projects
- Creative coding

## Selected Projects

Each project opens a case study in place (overview, purpose, what was built, stack and links). All project content lives in `src/pages/home/projects/projectsData.js`.

### Lendi Zone

A role-based ERP and learning management platform actively used by students and faculty.

### Gram Vikas

An offline-first AI companion bringing agriculture, healthcare and emergency support to rural communities. VIET Hackathon 2026 winner.

### OrbitGuard

An AI-assisted orbital safety platform: real satellite catalog data, SGP4 propagation, conjunction screening and 3D visualization.

### Verifly

Infrastructure project management for oil & gas, with AI verification and a 3D digital twin of the field.
